# WB JEMScN 2026 Official Exam Pattern & Comprehensive Syllabus Blueprint

This document details the layout, scoring methodology, and detailed, chapter-by-chapter curriculum blueprint established by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) for the **JEMScN 2026** examination.

# 1\. High-Level Paper Structure & Schedule

The Joint Entrance for Master of Science in Nursing (JEMScN) is a single-paper, offline OMR-based competitive test.

| Parameter | Specifications |
| :---- | :---- |
| Exam Duration | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) |
| Total Number of Questions | 100 Questions |
| Total Examination Marks | 100 Marks |
| Question Format | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options |
| Language Medium | English Only |
| Academic Base Standard | Graduate Level (B.Sc. Nursing / Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing) |

# 2\. Paper Section Weights & Structural Splits

The examination paper separates foundational sciences and professional nursing disciplines into two distinct parts. All questions follow a uniform single-category model.

| Paper Segment | Target Curricular Domain | Total Questions | Total Marks |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Part A** | Basic and Allied Sciences, Education, Administration & Research | 40 Q | 40 Marks |
| **Part B** | Core Clinical Nursing Subjects | 60 Q | 60 Marks |
| **Grand Total: Combined Single Paper** |  | **100 Q** | **100 Marks** |

**Critical Grader & Ranking Metric:** In the event of a tie-breaker during merit ranking, candidates with **higher total marks in Part B** are granted a higher standing rank, followed by candidates with fewer total negative marks.

# 3\. Scoring Methodology & Evaluation Rules

JEMScN 2026 does not feature multi-correct option parameters. The scoring template follows a strict single-choice evaluation routine:

* Number of Options Correct: Exactly **one** option out of four is correct.  
* Correct Response Reward: \+1.0 Mark  
* Incorrect Response Penalty: \-0.25 Mark (25% negative marking deduction).  
* Multiple Options Marked: Overlapping OMR bubble selections are considered incorrect, resulting in a penalty of \-0.25 Mark.  
* Unattempted Question: 0 Marks

# 4\. Detailed Subject, Chapter, and Topic Blueprint

## Part A: Basic, Allied, and Methodological Sciences (40 Questions / 40 Marks)

### 1\. Anatomy & Physiology

* **General & Systemic Anatomy:** Epithelial, connective, muscular, and nervous tissue structural setups. Skeletal systems, cranial configurations, thoracic cage, abdomen, pelvis, and central nervous system (CNS) pathway configurations.  
* **Systemic Physiology Dynamics:** Blood composition, clotting cascades, and plasma parameters. Cardiodynamics, respiratory volumes (TV, ERV, IRV), nephron filtration rates, gastrointestinal secretions, neuromuscular junction transmission, special senses, and endocrine feedback loops.  
* **Embryology & Histology:** Fundamental gametogenesis, germ layer differentiation, structural tissue changes, and microscopic anatomy layouts.

### 2\. Pathology & Genetics

* **General Pathology Processes:** Cellular adaptation (atrophy, hypertrophy, metaplasia), necrosis models, intracellular accumulations, and active wound-healing pathways.  
* **Systemic Pathology & Hematology:** Chronic inflammation, thrombosis, embolism tracking, systemic shock types, neoplastic classifications, and blood profile alterations (anemias, leukemias).  
* **Applied Human Genetics:** Mendelian laws of inheritance, chromosomal aberrations (Down, Turner, Klinefelter syndromes), molecular mutation patterns, gene mapping, and genetic counseling processes in nursing care.

### 3\. Microbiology & Pharmacology

* **Medical Bacteriology & Virology:** Bacterial cell structures, Gram staining validation protocols, and viral replication steps. Culture media setups, identification of clinical pathogens, and sample collections.  
* **Immunology & Environmental Hygiene:** Innate and adaptive immune systems, hypersensitivity reactions, immunoglobulin traits (IgG, IgM, IgA), sanitation controls, and infection metrics.  
* **Clinical Pharmacology:** Advanced drug classifications, pharmacodynamic receptor mechanisms, toxicity indicators, drug delivery models, and nurse-led calculation tracking for high-alert medications.

### 4\. Psychology & Sociology

* **Advanced Psychology:** Theories of human behavior, cognitive processes, memory storage models, defense mechanisms, and psychosocial adaptations to stress and clinical illness.  
* **Applied Sociology:** Social stratification systems, cultural impacts on wellness, family types, community structures, urban/rural demographics, and social issues (poverty, malnutrition).

### 5\. Nursing Administration & Management

* **Healthcare Administration:** Management theories, planning cycles, material resources handling, budgeting systems, and audit tools.  
* **Personnel Management:** Staffing calculations, job descriptions, performance appraisal loops, leadership styles, conflict resolution protocols, and Indian Nursing Council (INC) statutory guidelines.

### 6\. Nursing Education & Ethics

* **Educational Methodology:** Curriculum design steps, lesson planning frameworks, and teaching aids. Evaluation types (formative, summative, OSCE) and objective testing methods.  
* **Professional Ethics:** Code of Professional Conduct, consumer protection acts, legal accountability, and ethical dilemmas in clinical practice.

### 7\. Research Methodology & Biostatistics

* **Nursing Research Processes:** Research problem formulation, literature review steps, conceptual frameworks, quantitative and qualitative designs, sampling techniques, and tool testing.  
* **Biostatistical Applications:** Measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode), measures of dispersion (standard deviation, variance), parametric vs. non-parametric testing, data presentations, and standard error evaluations.

## Part B: Core Clinical Nursing Subjects (60 Questions / 60 Marks)

### 1\. Foundation of Nursing

* **Professional Core:** Historical evolution of nursing, nursing theories (Orem, Nightingale, Roy, Peplau), nursing process frameworks, and documentation standards.  
* **Clinical Safety & Therapeutics:** Advanced infection control protocols, medical and surgical asepsis guidelines, hospital waste segregation laws, patient safety benchmarks, fluid calculations, and emergency life support procedures.

### 2\. Medical-Surgical Nursing (Adult Health Nursing)

* **Critical & Perioperative Interventions:** Airway management, mechanical ventilation parameters, acid-base balancing, ABG analysis, fluid-electrolyte modifications, and pre-/intra-/post-operative nursing care.  
* **Systemic Nursing Care:** Advanced interventions for critical illnesses involving the cardiovascular (MI, heart failure), respiratory (COPD, ARDS), gastrointestinal (hepatic coma, pancreatitis), renal (acute/chronic failure), neurological (stroke, increased ICP), and musculoskeletal systems.  
* **Oncology & Immunological Care:** Cancer staging management, chemotherapy protocols, radiation protections, and nursing interventions for autoimmune and immunodeficient states.

### 3\. Pediatric Nursing (Child Health Nursing)

* **Developmental Milestones:** Age-specific anthropometric tracking and psychosexual/cognitive development milestones (Piaget, Erikson) from infancy through adolescence.  
* **Neonatal & Pediatric Emergencies:** High-risk newborn protocols, neonatal resuscitation algorithms, APGAR evaluations, low birth weight (LBW) management, and IMNCI guidelines.  
* **Congenital & Acute Pathologies:** Nursing care for structural malformations, pediatric infectious diseases, dehydration corrections, and nutritional deficiencies.

### 4\. Psychiatric Nursing (Mental Health Nursing)

* **Diagnostic Psychopathology:** Comprehensive Mental Status Examination (MSE), classification benchmarks, and therapeutic communication protocols.  
* **Major Mental Health Conditions:** Care pathways for schizophrenia, mood disorders, anxiety syndromes, personality disorders, substance withdrawal, and geriatric mental health conditions.  
* **Psychiatric Modalities:** Somatic therapies (ECT preparation and monitoring), psychopharmacological tracking, lithium level monitoring, and behavioral modification practices.

### 5\. Obstetrical & Gynecological Nursing

* **Maternal & Fetal Surveillance:** Physiological changes during pregnancy, antenatal screening schedules, fetal monitoring techniques (NST, biophysical profiles), and obstetric calculations.  
* **Intrapartum & Postpartum Management:** Labor stage progression monitoring, partograph tracking, active third-stage labor management (AMTSL), and care for complications (eclampsia, postpartum hemorrhage, obstructed labor).  
* **Gynecological Abnormalities:** Nursing management for reproductive tract infections, menstrual irregularities, uterine displacements, and infertility protocols.

### 6\. Community Health Nursing

* **Public Health & Epidemiology:** Epidemiological tracking methods, calculation of demographic rates (MMR, IMR, CBR), and environmental health controls.  
* **National Policies & Infrastructure:** Primary healthcare delivery networks (sub-centers, PHCs, CHCs), implementation protocols for national health programs, vaccine cold-chain maintenance, and family planning strategies.
