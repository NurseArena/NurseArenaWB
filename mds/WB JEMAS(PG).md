# WB JEMAS(PG) 2026 Official Exam Pattern & Comprehensive Syllabus Blueprint

This document details the uniform exam structure, scoring rules, and precise subject-wise marks distribution templates across different course categories established by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) for the **JEMAS(PG) 2026** examination cycle.

## 1\. High-Level Paper Structure & Schedule

The Joint Entrance Management Aptitude Test for Post Graduate (JEMAS-PG) is an offline, pen-and-paper (OMR-based) common entrance test. Each specific postgraduate course tracking stream is evaluated via its own separate dedicated question paper bundle.

| Parameter | Specifications |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Exam Duration** | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) |
| **Total Number of Questions** | 100 Questions (Per Course Paper Bundle) |
| **Total Examination Marks** | 100 Marks |
| **Question Format** | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options |
| **Language Medium** | English Only |
| **Academic Base Standard** | Relevant Qualifying Undergraduate Bachelor Degree Course |

## 2\. Evaluation & Marking Scheme

The evaluation system across all distinct question papers in JEMAS(PG) uses a single-correct matching protocol:

* **Number of Options Correct:** Exactly one option out of four.  
* **Correct Response Reward:** \+1.0 Mark  
* **Incorrect Response Penalty:** \-0.25 Mark (25% negative marking penalty).  
* **Multiple Options Marked:** Flagged as an incorrect response, resulting in a \-0.25 Mark deduction.  
* **Unattempted Question:** 0 Marks

### H. 100-Question Single Discipline Papers (Dip Diet, DHPE, DHS, FPM, MSLP, M.Sc. CCS, M.Sc. OTS, M.Sc. PS)

For these specific technical degrees, the paper is not structurally sub-partitioned. It consists of a single \*\*100 Question / 100 Marks\*\* assessment focusing wholly on the respective underlying core qualification courses:

* **Dip Diet (Diploma in Dietetics):** 100% Core Bachelor course syllabus of Basic Nutrition, Physiology, Chemistry, and Basic Food Science.  
* **DHPE (Diploma in Health Promotion & Education):** 100% Core Bachelor syllabus of Basic Public Health Sciences.  
* **DHS (Diploma in Health Statistics):** 100% Higher Secondary (Class XII) standard Mathematics alongside core fundamentals of Statistics.  
* **FPM (Fellowship in Pain Management):** 100% MBBS Standard questions covering Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, Psychiatry, Anesthesiology, Physical Medicine, Orthopedics, General Medicine, Rheumatology, Oncology, and Community Medicine related specifically to Pain management.  
* **MSLP (M.Sc. in Speech-Language Pathology):** 100% Introduction to Speech-Language Pathology, Childhood Communication Disorders, Articulation/Phonological Disorders, Voice/Laryngectomy, Fluency Variations, and Adult Neuromotor Speech Disorders.  
* **M.Sc. CCS / OTS / PS / MB / MM / PGDDRM:** 100% Focused on the highly unique clinical specialization track metrics as taught at the corresponding Bachelors level (Critical Care Technology, Operation Theatre Technology, Perfusion Technology, Medical Microbiology, and Disability Rehabilitation Management).

# 4\. Detailed Subject, Chapter, and Topic Blueprint

## 1\. Master in Hospital Administration (MHA) Syllabus

### Chapter 1: Quantitative Aptitude & Arithmetic (10 Questions / 10 Marks)

* **Topic 1.1: Core Numerical Systems**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Prime and composite numbers, divisibility tests, remainders, simplification, fractions, decimals, and properties of HCF and LCM.  
* **Topic 1.2: Business Mathematics**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Ratios, direct and inverse proportions, percentages, profit and loss calculations, discount structures, partnerships, and simple vs. compound interest equations.  
* **Topic 1.3: Operational Dynamics & Work Metrics**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Time and work rates, pipe and cistern systems, speed, distance, average velocity, and relative motion problems.  
* **Topic 1.4: Statistical Data Interpretation**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Reading and interpreting data from tables, line graphs, bar charts, pie charts, and calculating averages or percentages from datasets.

### Chapter 2: Logical & Analytical Reasoning (20 Questions / 20 Marks)

* **Topic 2.1: Alphanumeric & Series Progression**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Number series, letter coding, pattern tracking, missing character problems, and alphanumeric arrangements.  
* **Topic 2.2: Relational & Spatial Tracking**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Family blood relation maps, directional vectors (cardinal turns, distance tracking), and linear or circular seating arrangements.  
* **Topic 2.3: Verbal & Syllogistic Logic**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Standard categorical syllogisms, Venn diagram intersections, statement-assumption mappings, and cause-and-effect reasoning.

### Chapter 3: General Science Foundation (30 Questions / 30 Marks)

* **Topic 3.1: Physics Principles**  
  * *Sub-topics:* SI units, dimensional formulas, mechanics (laws of motion, force equations), work, power, kinetic and potential energy, thermodynamics (heat transfer mechanisms), light (reflection, refraction, lens behavior), and fundamental electrical networks (Ohm’s law).  
* **Topic 3.2: Chemical Properties**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Matter states, atomic structure, the periodic table (group trends, electronegativity), chemical bonding models, characteristics of acids, bases, and salts, and basic organic carbon compounds.  
* **Topic 3.3: Biological Systems & Human Physiology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Plant vs. animal cell anatomy, mitosis and meiosis cell cycles, vital human organ systems (circulatory mechanics, respiratory gas transfer, digestive track layers, central nervous system, endocrine glands), and nutritional biochemistry (vitamins, minerals, and common deficiency states).

### Chapter 4: English Language & Syntax Proficiency (20 Questions / 20 Marks)

* **Topic 4.1: Grammatical Corrections**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Subject-verb agreement, tense modifications, prepositions, articles, and active-to-passive voice conversions.  
* **Topic 4.2: Vocabulary & Textual Cohesion**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Synonyms, antonyms, idioms, cloze test completions, sentence jumbles, and contextual reading comprehension.

### Chapter 5: General Knowledge & Healthcare Infrastructure (20 Questions / 20 Marks)

* **Topic 5.1: Constitutional Foundations**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles of State Policy, and the structure of Parliament.  
* **Topic 5.2: Indian Healthcare Ecosystem**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) organization, structure of sub-centers, PHCs, CHCs, and basic administrative protocols of public hospitals.

## 2\. Master of Public Health (MPH) Syllabus

### Chapter 1: Section A — Biological & Medical Sciences (35 Questions / 35 Marks)

* **Topic 1.1: General Biological Sciences**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Cellular structure, classification of plant and animal kingdoms, basic inheritance principles, and molecular biology basics.  
* **Topic 1.2: Medical Specialties & Clinical Basics**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Basics of human anatomy, organ system physiology, clinical pharmacology parameters, common dental diseases, and fundamentals of alternative medicine streams (AYUSH).  
* **Topic 1.3: Basic Nursing & Pharmaceutical Frameworks**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Infection control practices, patient care models, drug classification systems, and basic immunization mechanics.

### Chapter 2: Section B — Social Sciences & Social Determinants of Health (35 Questions / 35 Marks)

* **Topic 2.1: Anthropological & Sociological Perspectives**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Social structure, family systems, cultural beliefs regarding illness, community group dynamics, and methods of social research.  
* **Topic 2.2: Health Economics & Development Studies**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Supply and demand principles in healthcare, health financing models, indicators of rural poverty, and economic challenges of health planning.  
* **Topic 2.3: Environmental & Social Determinants**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Urbanization health impacts, clean water supply parameters, waste sanitation systems, socioeconomic status scaling (Kuppuswamy/Prasad scales), and gender barriers to care.

### Chapter 3: Section C — Biostatistics, Numerical Ability & Public Health GK (30 Questions / 30 Marks)

* **Topic 3.1: Quantitative Methods & Biostatistics**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Meaning and types of data, methods of data collection, measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode), measures of dispersion (variance, standard deviation), sampling methodologies, and basic hypothesis testing.  
* **Topic 3.2: Epidemiology & Vital Registration**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Principles of epidemiology, epidemiological triad, disease transmission models, natural history of disease, calculating incidence and prevalence, and computing birth/death rates (MMR, IMR, CBR).  
* **Topic 3.3: National Health Policies & Current Affairs**  
  * *Sub-topics:* National Health Mission (NHM) structures, Ayushman Bharat, current public health trends, global health warnings, and national disease elimination timelines.

## 3\. M.Sc. Medical Laboratory Technology (M.Sc. MLT) Syllabus

### Chapter 1: Clinical Biochemistry (35 Questions / 35 Marks)

* **Topic 1.1: Macromolecular Biochemistry & Enzymology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Amino acid structures, protein folding dynamics, enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten equations), and mechanism models of enzyme inhibition.  
* **Topic 1.2: Metabolic Pathways & Regulation**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Glycolysis, TCA cycle, glycogen synthesis/breakdown, beta-oxidation of fatty acids, urea cycle, and associated metabolic storage disorders.  
* **Topic 1.3: Clinical Analytics & Diagnostics**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Liver and renal function test panels, cardiac markers, plasma lipid profiling, blood gas values, and automation in biochemistry analyzers.

### Chapter 2: Medical Microbiology & Immunochemistry (35 Questions / 35 Marks)

* **Topic 2.1: Systematic Bacteriology & Mycology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Staining protocols (Gram, Ziehl-Neelsen), bacterial cell walls, culture media selection, and identification profiles of common pathogens (Staphylococcus, Enterobacteriaceae).  
* **Topic 2.2: Virology & Parasitology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Viral cultivation techniques, diagnostic molecular assays (PCR), and the lifecycle and laboratory identification of blood and intestinal parasites (Plasmodium, Entamoeba).  
* **Topic 2.3: Immunochemistry & Serology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Innate and adaptive immunity pathways, structure of antigens and antibodies, hypersensitivity models, and performing diagnostic serological tests (ELISA, Widal, Western Blot).

### Chapter 3: Hematology & Blood Banking (30 Questions / 30 Marks)

* **Topic 3.1: Core Hematology & Pathologies**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Erythropoiesis steps, morphological classification of anemias, leukopoiesis, diagnostic features of leukemias, and manual vs. automated cell counting methods.  
* **Topic 3.2: Coagulation Studies & Bone Marrow Analysis**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Physiology of hemostasis, processing coagulation assays (PT, APTT, INR), and bone marrow collection, staining, and biopsy preparation techniques.  
* **Topic 3.3: Blood Banking & Immunohematology**  
  * *Sub-topics:* Major blood group systems (ABO, Rh), cross-matching compatibility protocols, donor screening standards, component preparation methods, and managing transfusion reactions.
