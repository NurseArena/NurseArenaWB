# WB ANM & GNM 2026 Official Exam Pattern & Syllabus Blueprint

## 1\. High-Level Paper Structure & Blueprint

The Auxiliary Nursing & Midwifery (ANM) and General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) common entrance test is administered as a single-paper, offline OMR-based exam by the WBJEEB.

| Parameter | Specifications |
| :---- | :---- |
| Exam Duration | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) |
| Total Questions | 100 Questions |
| Total Examination Marks | 115 Marks |
| Question Format | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options |
| Language Medium | Bilingual (English & Bengali) (Note: Basic English and Logical Reasoning sections are in English only) |

## 2\. Subject-Wise Question Pattern & Marks Distribution

The paper consists of 6 subjects. Only Life Science and Physical Science contain advanced Category II questions. All other subjects consist strictly of Category I questions.

| Subject | Category I (1 Mark each) | Category II (2 Marks each) | Total Questions | Total Marks | Curriculum Standard |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Life Science | 30 Q | 10 Q | 40 | 50 | Class 10 Level |
| Physical Science | 15 Q | 5 Q | 20 | 25 | Class 10 Level |
| Basic English | 15 Q | — | 15 | 15 | Class 12 Level |
| Mathematics | 10 Q | — | 10 | 10 | Class 10 Level |
| General Knowledge | 10 Q | — | 10 | 10 | Class 12 Level |
| Logical Reasoning | 5 Q | — | 5 | 5 | Class 12 Level |
| **Total Split** | **85 Questions** | **15 Questions** | **100** | **115** | — |

## 3\. Category-Wise Evaluation & Marking Schemes

### Category I (Single Correct Option)

* Correct Response Reward: \+1.0 Mark  
* Incorrect Response Penalty: \-0.25 Mark (25% negative marking).  
* Multiple Options Marked: Any question containing more than one marked bubble on the OMR sheet is automatically flagged as incorrect and penalized with a \-0.25 Mark deduction.  
* Unattempted Question: 0 Marks

### \#\#\# Category II (Multi-Correct Options)

* Correct Response Reward: \+2.0 Marks (Awarded only if all correct options are checked and zero incorrect options are marked).  
* Incorrect Response Penalty: 0 Marks (No negative marking).  
* The "Any Wrong Selection" Rule: Marking any incorrect option immediately invalidates the entire question score, reducing it to 0 Marks, even if some correct options were selected alongside it.  
* Partial Marking Formula: If a candidate marks only a subset of the correct choices and does not select any incorrect option, they receive proportional marks using the following formula:

Score \= 2 × (Number of Correct Options Marked / Total Number of Actually Correct Options Available)

## 4\. Detailed Subject-Wise Syllabus Matrix

### Science & Mathematics Core (Class 10 Standard)

#### Life Science (Category I & II)

* Cellular Organisation & Processes: Cell structure, organelles, cell division (mitosis, meiosis), and basic fundamental biomolecules.  
* Systems Physiology (Animal & Human): Digestion, respiration, circulatory systems, excretory mechanisms, nervous system coordination, endocrine glands, and hormone functions.  
* Plant Physiology: Photosynthesis, respiration, transpiration, plant growth regulators, and tropic movements.  
* Inheritance & Genetics: Mendelian laws of inheritance, mono/dihybrid crosses, common genetic disorders, and DNA/RNA basic traits.  
* Health, Disease & Applied Biology: Structure of pathogenetic microbes, immunology fundamentals, common infectious and non-infectious human diseases, vector control, and prevention.  
* Ecological Principles: Ecosystem components, energy flow, food chains, biodiversity conservation, population dynamics, and environmental pollution mitigation.

#### Physical Science (Category I & II)

* Measurement & Matter: Systems of units, physical quantities, structural properties of solid/liquid/gas states, separation of mixtures, and solution properties.  
* Force, Motion & Energy: Newton’s laws of motion, linear momentum, velocity-time graphs, work, power, kinetic and potential energy metrics, sound wave propagation, and structural dynamics.  
* Thermal & Gaseous Behaviour: Ideal gas behaviors, Boyle’s and Charles’s laws, kinetic theory interpretations, thermal expansion phenomena, and heat transfer.  
* Light & Optics: Reflection, refraction, spherical lenses, lens formulas, dispersion, and human eye visual anomalies.  
* Current Electricity & Chemical Effects: Ohm’s law, electrical components, resistance networks, Joule's heating effects, chemical effects of current, electrolysis, and basic magnetism.  
* Chemical Calculations & Atomic Chemistry: Mole concept, atomic weight, chemical equations, atomic structure, periodic classification trends, ionic/covalent bonding, and industrial inorganic chemistry.

#### Mathematics (Category I Only)

* Arithmetic: Natural numbers, integers, fractions, decimals, ratios, proportions, percentages, profit and loss, simple interest, and partnership business.  
* Algebra: Laws of exponents, indices, surds, and single-variable linear and quadratic equations.  
* Geometry & Mensuration: Core geometric theorems of triangles and circles, area and perimeter calculations, volume/surface configurations of cylinders, cones, and spheres.

### Language, Reasoning & Aptitude Core (Class 12 Standard)

#### Basic English (Category I Only)

* Grammar Architecture: Tense rules, articles, prepositions, voice transitions, and direct/indirect speech transformations.  
* Syntax & Error Isolation: Subject-verb agreement, spotting errors, sentence corrections, and clause restructuring.  
* Vocabulary: Synonyms, antonyms, homophones/homonyms, idioms and phrases, cloze tests, and one-word substitutions.  
* Comprehension Skills: Reading comprehension passages, sentence rearrangement, and paragraph jumbles.

#### General Knowledge (Category I Only)

* History & Geography: Landmark events in Indian history, national freedom movements, and physical/economic geography layout of India and West Bengal.  
* Polity & Economy: Overview of the Indian Constitution, governing bodies, fundamental rights, and rudimentary pillars of the Indian economy.  
* General Science & Static GK: Famous discoveries, inventions, national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, state symbols, and major cultural folk arts.  
* Current Affairs: Recent developments in public health policies, healthcare schemes, awards, honors, global events, and sports milestones.

#### Logical Reasoning (Category I Only)

* Verbal Logic: Alphanumeric series, coding-decoding structures, blood relation paths, direction sense tracking, analogies, and logical word classifications.  
* Analytical & Non-Verbal Logic: Venn diagrams, syllogisms, pattern completion, matrix tracking, mirror images, embedded figures, and basic scheduling puzzles.
