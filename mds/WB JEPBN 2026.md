
# WB JEPBN 2026 Official Exam Pattern & Syllabus Blueprint



# 1\. High-Level Paper Structure & Schedule

The Joint Entrance Examination for Post-Basic B.Sc. Nursing (JEPBN) is conducted as a single-paper, offline OMR-based test by the WBJEEB.

| Parameter | Specifications |  
| **Exam Duration** | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) |  
| **Total Number of Questions** | 100 Questions |  
| **Total Examination Marks** | 100 Marks |  
| **Question Format** | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options |  
| **Language Medium** | English Only |

# 2\. Paper-Wise Question Pattern & Marks Distribution

The examination paper is divided into two distinct logical segments (Part A and Part B) based on the General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) curriculum core. Every single question across both sections falls under a single uniform marking category.

| Paper Segment | Target Curricular Domain | Total Questions | Total Marks |  
| **Part A** | Biological & Social Sciences (Foundational) | 40 Q | 40 Marks |  
| **Part B** | Applied Nursing Sciences (Advanced Disciplines) | 60 Q | 60 Marks |  
| **Grand Total** | **Combined Single Paper** | **100 Q** | **100 Marks** |

# 3\. Evaluation & Marking Scheme

Unlike other WBJEEB exams, JEPBN does not utilize a complex multi-correct option tier. The entire sheet follows a standardized single-correct valuation algorithm.

\* **Number of Options Correct:** Exactly **one** option out of four is correct.  
\* **Correct Response Reward:** \+1.0 Mark  
\* **Incorrect Response Penalty:** \-0.25 Mark (25% negative marking deduction).  
\* **Multiple Options Marked:** If a candidate shades more than one bubble for a question on the OMR sheet, it is flagged as incorrect and incurs a penalty of \-0.25 Mark.  
\* **Unattempted Question:** 0 Marks

# 4\. Detailed Subject-Wise Syllabus Matrix

The exam content is drawn directly from the standard GNM course curriculum and is systematically partitioned into the following areas:

# Part A: Foundational Sciences (40 Questions / 40 Marks)

## 1\. Anatomy

* **Chapter 1: Skeletal & Muscular Architecture**

    \*   Classification, structural anatomy, and axial/appendicular divisions of bone networks.  
    \*   Joint types (synovial, fibrous, cartilaginous) and localized movement mechanics.  
    \*   Microscopic anatomy of skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle fibers.

* **Chapter 2: Cardio-Respiratory & Visceral Systems**

    \*   Structural chambers, valve mechanics, and coronary arterial pathways of the heart.  
    \*   Anatomy of the upper and lower respiratory tracts, trachea, bronchi, and alveoli partitions.  
    \*   Gastrointestinal layer structures (mucosa, submucosa), liver, pancreas, and biliary trees.

* **Chapter 3: Neuro-Urinary & Endocrine Layout**

    \*   Central Nervous System structural segments (cerebrum, cerebellum, brainstem, spinal cord).  
    \*   Nephron micro-anatomy, renal cortex, medulla, ureters, bladder, and urethral sphincters.  
    \*   Anatomical positions of the pituitary, thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal, and pineal glands.

## 2\. Physiology

* **Chapter 1: Hematology & Cardiovascular Dynamics**

    \*   Plasma composition, RBC/WBC/platelet life cycles, and coagulation cascade steps.  
    \*   Cardiac cycle phases, electrical conduction pathways (SA node, AV node, Purkinje system).  
    \*   Regulation of arterial blood pressure, stroke volume, and peripheral resistance.

* **Chapter 2: Respiratory, Digestive & Renal Mechanics**

    \*   Gas exchange mechanisms, partial pressure gradients, and oxyhemoglobin dissociation variables.  
    \*   Enzymatic breakdown pathways (salivary, gastric, pancreatic, intestinal) and absorption sites.  
    \*   Glomerular filtration rate (GFR) regulation, countercurrent multipliers, and micturition reflexes.

* **Chapter 3: Endocrine Signalling & Reproduction**

    \*   Hormonal feedback loops, target organ receptors, and metabolic homoeostasis pathways.  
    \*   Spermatogenesis, oogenesis, phases of the menstrual cycle, and hormonal control mechanics.

## 3\. Microbiology

* **Chapter 1: Bacteriology & Virological Strains**

    \*   Bacterial morphology, cell wall structural variants (Gram-positive vs. Gram-negative protocols).  
    \*   Viral replication cycles, structural capsids, and pathogenic human classification groupings.

* **Chapter 2: Sterilization & Infection Control**

    \*   Physical control methods (autoclaving parameters, hot air oven metrics, pasteurization).  
    \*   Chemical disinfectants, antiseptic grading, and institutional CSSD workflow steps.

* **Chapter 3: Immunology & Diagnostic Serology**

    \*   Innate vs. adaptive immunity channels, cellular vs. humoral pathways.  
Immunoglobulin configurations (IgG, IgM, IgA, IgE, IgD), antigen-antibody tests (ELISA, Widal, PCR).

## 4\. Pathology

* **Chapter 1: Cellular Adaptation & Tissue Injury**

    \*   Mechanisms of atrophy, hypertrophy, hyperplasia, metaplasia, dysplasia, and apoptosis.  
    \*   Ischemic cell death parameters, free radical damage, and cellular necrosis pathways.

* **Chapter 2: Inflammation & Hemodynamic Disorders**

    \*   Acute vs. chronic vascular and cellular responses, chemical mediators, and exudate formation.  
    \*   Edema mechanisms, thrombosis formation, embolism variants, and stages of septic/hypovolemic shock.

* **Chapter 3: Neoplasia & Systemic Healing**

    \*   Benign vs. malignant tumor characteristics, staging benchmarks, and metastatic paths.  
    \*   Primary and secondary intention healing of cutaneous and deep visceral wounds.

## 5\. Pharmacology

* **Chapter 1: Pharmacokinetics & Pharmacodynamics**

    \*   Drug absorption profiles, first-pass metabolism, bioavailability, and clearance kinetics.  
    \*   Receptor-binding mechanisms, agonists, antagonists, therapeutic indexes, and toxic thresholds.

* **Chapter 2: Systemic Therapeutics**

    \*   Autonomic drugs, cardiovascular agents (antihypertensives, inotropes, anticoagulants).  
    \*   Central nervous system agents (analgesics, anesthetics, sedatives, anticonvulsants).

* **Chapter 3: Chemotherapy & Antibiotic Stewardship**

    \*   Classification of beta-lactams, aminoglycosides, macrolides, fluoroquinolones, and antifungals.  
    \*   Mechanisms of microbial resistance development and high-risk drug monitoring criteria.

## 6\. Nutrition

* **Chapter 1: Macronutrient & Micronutrient Energetics**

    \*   Metabolic yields of proteins, carbohydrates, and lipids; BMR formulas and calculations.  
Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) and water-soluble (B-complex, C) vitamin absorption and dynamics.

* **Chapter 2: Therapeutic Dietetics & Deficiencies**

    \*   Formulation of diabetic, renal, low-sodium, high-protein, and liquid/enteric diets.  
    \*   Clinical signs of Protein-Energy Malnutrition (PEM \- Marasmus, Kwashiorkor) and trace mineral deficiencies.

## 7\. Psychology

* **Chapter 1: Cognitive Processes & Behavior**

    \*   Theories of learning (classical/operant conditioning) and memory retention frameworks.  
    \*   Attention, perception mechanics, structural illusions, and emotional intelligence variables.

* **Chapter 2: Personality & Defense Mechanisms**

    \*   Id, Ego, Superego dynamics, personality typology testing, and maladaptive behaviors.  
    \*   Ego defense mechanics: Projection, rationalization, sublimation, displacement, regression.

## 8\. Sociology

* **Chapter 1: Social Groups & Cultural Frameworks**

    \*   Primary and secondary groups, crowd dynamics, socialization steps, and community structures.  
    \*   Impact of caste, class, religious systems, and family structures on healthcare access.

* **Chapter 2: Social Pathology & Healthcare Interfaces**

    \*   Sociological parameters of poverty, substance abuse, domestic violence, and population growth.

# Part B: Applied Nursing Sciences (60 Questions / 60 Marks)

## 1\. Fundamentals of Nursing

* **Chapter 1: Professional Paradigms & Communication**

    \*   Nursing process steps (Assessment, Diagnosis, Planning, Implementation, Evaluation).  
    \*   Therapeutic communication barriers, documentation parameters, and legal-ethical models.

* **Chapter 2: Clinical Intervention & Vital Metrics**

    \*   Precision mapping of temperature, pulse, respiration, and blood pressure errors.  
    \*   Medication administration protocols (Enteral, Parenteral, IV fluid rate calculation metrics).  
    \*   Aseptic cleaning, wound dressing, catheterization, and bed-sore prevention stages.

## 2\. Medical-Surgical Nursing

* **Chapter 1: Perioperative Care & Critical Fluid Management**

    \*   Pre-op validation checksheets, intra-op monitoring, and post-anesthesia scoring.  
    \*   Acid-base imbalances (Respiratory/Metabolic Acidosis and Alkalosis) and ABG interpretation.  
    \*   Shock management algorithms, blood transfusion protocols, and central venous pressure tracking.

* **Chapter 2: Cardiovascular, Respiratory & Gastrointestinal Pathologies**

    \*   Nursing care for MI, Heart Failure, CAD, Hypertension, and Dysrhythmias.  
    \*   Management of COPD, Asthma, Pneumonia, ARDS, and Chest Drainage Systems (UWCD).  
    \*   Interventions for Peptic Ulcer disease, Cirrhosis, Pancreatitis, and Inflammatory Bowel Diseases.

* **Chapter 3: Renal, Endocrine & Neurological Alterations**

    \*   Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) stages, Hemodialysis maintenance, and AKI phases.  
    \*   Management of Type 1/2 Diabetes, DKA, Hyperthyroidism, and Hypothyroidism.  
    \*   Stroke care pathways, Increased Intracranial Pressure (ICP) signs, and Glasgow Coma Scale evaluations.

## 3\. Pediatric Nursing (Child Health)

* **Chapter 1: Growth & Developmental Milestones**

    \*   Anthropometric tracking indicators (weight, height, head circumferences) from birth to adolescence.  
    \*   Developmental steps (Piaget, Erikson, Freud) and neonatal reflex integration metrics.

* **Chapter 2: Neonatal Care & Congenital Malformations**

    \*   Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC) protocols, neonatal resuscitation steps, and APGAR scoring parameters.  
    \*   Management of Cleft Lip/Palate, Tracheoesophageal Fistula, Hirschsprung’s Disease, and Spina Bifida.

* **Chapter 3: Childhood Illnesses & Integrated Protocols**

    \*   IMNCI diagnostic protocols for respiratory infections, dehydration grading, and severe malnutrition.

## 4\. Psychiatric Nursing (Mental Health)

* **Chapter 1: Psychopathology & Diagnostic Evaluation**

    \*   Mental Status Examination (MSE) structure, behavioral tracking, and thought content analysis.  
    \*   Classification systems (ICD/DSM) for checking signs of mood, anxiety, and thought disorders.

* **Chapter 2: Major Psychiatric Conditions**

    \*   Nursing protocols for Schizophrenia, Bipolar Affective Disorder (Mania/Depression), and OCD.  
    \*   Substance withdrawal management (alcohol, opioids) and organic brain syndrome (Delirium/Dementia) care.

* **Chapter 3: Psychiatric Therapeutics**

    \*   Side-effect management for antipsychotics, antidepressants, lithium toxicity tracking, and ECT setup rules.

## 5\. Obstetrical Nursing (Maternal Health)

* **Chapter 1: Antenatal Assessment & Fetal Surveillance**

    \*   Physiological modifications of pregnancy, obstetric calculations (EDD, Gravida, Para splits).  
    \*   Fetal assessment steps (USG profiles, Non-Stress Test, contraction monitoring).

* **Chapter 2: Intrapartum Mechanics & Complications**

    \*   Stages of normal labor, partograph plotting, and active management of the third stage (AMTSL).  
    \*   Interventions for Preeclampsia, Eclampsia, Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH), and Obstructed Labor.

* **Chapter 3: Postnatal Care & Neonatal Stabilization**

    \*   Lactation mechanics, puerperal sepsis evaluation, and immediate management of the low-birth-weight neonate.

## 6\. Community Health Nursing

* **Chapter 1: Epidemiology & Demographic Controls**

    \*   Epidemiological triads, dynamics of disease transmission, and calculation of incidence/prevalence metrics.  
    \*   Vital statistics analysis (Maternal Mortality Rate, Infant Mortality Rate, Crude Death Rate calculations).

* **Chapter 2: National Health Schemes & Policy Structures**

    \*   Administrative frameworks of sub-centers, PHCs, CHCs, and urban health infrastructures.  
    \*   National implementation protocols for NHM, RCH, TB-Elimination (NTEP), and Vector-Borne Control.

* **Chapter 3: Family Planning & Environmental Sanitation**

    \*   Contraceptive mechanism indexing, cold-chain maintenance protocols, and safe solid-waste management.
