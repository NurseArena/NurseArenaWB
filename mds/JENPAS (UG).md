## 1\. High-Level Paper Structure & Blueprint

JENPAS(UG) 2026 is an offline, OMR-based entrance examination administered by the WBJEEB. The test is structured into two separate papers targeting distinct undergraduate professional streams.

| Parameter | Paper I (B.Sc. Nursing & Allied Health Sciences) | Paper II (Bachelor of Hospital Administration \- BHA) |  
| Duration | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) | 1.5 Hours (90 Minutes) |  
| Total Questions | 100 Questions | 100 Questions |  
| Total Marks | 115 Marks | 115 Marks |  
| Question Type | 4-Option MCQs (Category I & II) | 4-Option MCQs (Category I & II) |  
| Medium | Bilingual (English & Bengali)\* | Bilingual (English & Bengali)\* |

Note: Sections for Basic English, Logical Reasoning, and General Knowledge are provided in English only.

## 3\. Category Evaluation & Marking Schemes

### Category I (Single Correct Option)

Number of Options Correct: Exactly one option out of four is correct.  
Correct Response: \+1.0 Mark  
Incorrect Response: \-0.25 Mark (25% negative marking penalty).  
Multiple Options Marked: Handled as an incorrect response, resulting in a \-0.25 Mark penalty.  
Unattempted: 0 Marks

### Category II (Multi-Correct Options)

Number of Options Correct: One or more options can be correct out of four.  
Correct Response: \+2.0 Marks (Only if all correct choices are marked and zero incorrect choices are marked).  
Incorrect Response: 0 Marks (No negative marking).  
The "Any Wrong Selection" Penalty: If a candidate marks any option that is incorrect, the entire score for that question reduces instantly to 0 Marks, even if some correct choices were also highlighted.  
Partial Marking Formula: If a candidate selects only a subset of correct choices without checking any incorrect options, marks are awarded proportionately using the expression:

$$\\text{Score} \= 2 \\times \\left( \\frac{\\text{Number of Correct Options Marked}}{\\text{Total Number of Actually Correct Options Available}} \\right)$$

## 4\. Subject-Wise Syllabus Matrix

### Paper I Syllabus (Class 11 & Class 12 Level)

#### Physics (Category I & II)

\* Units, Dimensions, and Error Measurement.  
\* Kinematics, Vectors, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy, and Power.  
\* Motion of Systems of Particles, Rigid Body Dynamics, and Gravitation.  
\* Properties of Bulk Matter (Elasticity, Viscosity, Surface Tension).  
\* Thermodynamics, Behavior of Perfect Gas, and Kinetic Theory.  
\* Oscillations, Simple Harmonic Motion, and Mechanical Waves.  
\* Electrostatics, Electric Fields, Current Electricity, and Kirchhoff's Laws.  
\* Magnetic Effects of Current, Alternating Currents (AC), and Electromagnetic Induction.  
\* Ray Optics, Wave Optics, Dual Nature of Matter, Atoms, and Nuclei.  
\* Electronic Devices: Semiconductors, $p$-$n$ Junction Diodes, and Basic Logic Gates.

#### Chemistry (Category I & II)

\* Physical: Core Concepts of Chemistry, Structure of Atom, States of Matter, Chemical Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Redox Reactions, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, and Surface Chemistry.  
\* Inorganic: Classification of Elements and Periodicity, Chemical Bonding, Molecular Structure, s-Block Elements, p-Block Elements, d-and-f-Block Elements, Coordination Compounds, and Principles of Isolation of Elements.  
\* Organic: General Organic Chemistry (GOC), Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes, Haloarenes, Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers, Aldehydes, Ketones, Carboxylic Acids, Amines, Biomolecules, Polymers, and Chemistry in Everyday Life.

#### Biology (Category I & II)

\* Diversity in Living Organisms, Classification of Plants and Animals.  
\* Structural Organisation in Plants and Animals (Anatomy and Morphology).  
\* Cell Structure, Cell Division, and Biomolecules.  
\* Plant Physiology: Photosynthesis, Respiration, Plant Growth, and Hormones.  
\* Human Physiology: Digestion, Breathing, Circulation, Excretion, Locomotion, and Nervous/Endocrine Coordination.  
\* Reproduction in Flowering Plants and Human Reproductive Systems.  
\* Genetics and Evolution: Mendelian Inheritance, Molecular Basis of Inheritance, Mutation, and Modern Evolutionary Theory.  
\* Biology and Human Welfare: Health, Immunology, Plant Breeding, and Microbes.  
\* Biotechnology Principles, DNA Recombination Processes, and Applications.  
\* Ecology and Environment: Ecosystems, Population Dynamics, Biodiversity, and Environmental Pollution.

#### Basic English (Category I Only)

\* Articles, Prepositions, Tense, Voice Change, and Narration.  
\* Subject-Verb Agreement, Error Detection, and Sentence Correction.  
\* Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms and Phrases, and One-word Substitutions.  
\* Reading Comprehension, Cloze Tests, and Ordering of Sentences.

#### Health Aptitude (Category I Only)

Human Body: Basic anatomy of major skeletal, muscular, circulatory, nervous, respiratory, and digestive organ systems and their primary functions.  
Nutrition: Components of a balanced diet, macro/micronutrients, vitamins, minerals, and deficiency-related clinical conditions (malnutrition, anemia).  
Hygiene & Public Health: Personal hygiene practices, community sanitation, modes of transmission of communicable vectors, and primary disease prevention techniques.

### \#\#\# Paper II Syllabus (BHA Specific)

#### Physical Science (Category I & II — Class 10 Level)

Physics Domain: Standard Mechanics, Laws of Motion, Work/Power/Energy relationships, Behaviour of Sound waves, Reflection and Refraction of Light, Current Electricity, Electromagnetism, Heat transfer, and Basic Modern Physics.  
Chemistry Domain: Classification of Matter, Atomic Structure, Chemical Reactions and Equations, Properties of Acids, Bases, and Salts, General characteristics of Metals and Non-Metals, Carbon and its organic chemical compounds, and Periodic Table Trends.

#### Mathematics (Category I & II — Class 10 Level)

Arithmetic: Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Uniform Rate of Growth/Decay, Partnership Business.  
Algebra: Real Numbers, Laws of Indices, Quadratic Equations with one variable, Ratio and Proportion, Quadratic Surds, and Variations.  
Geometry & Mensuration: Theorems related to Circle geometry, Tangents, Triangles, Similarity, Pyramids, Spheres, Cones, Cylinders, and real-life problems containing regular multi-solid objects.  
Trigonometry & Statistics: Trigonometric Ratios, Basic Identities, Heights and Distances, Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Ogive, Mode).

#### General Knowledge (Category I Only — Class 12 Level)

\* Geography (Physical, Indian, and Economic Geography parameters).  
\* Indian History and Indian National Freedom Movement milestones.  
\* Indian Polity (The Constitution, Governance structures, Panchayati Raj, and Parliamentary rules).  
\* Indian Economy foundations, State and Central Budget systems, and national fiscal policies.  
\* General Science advancements, Inventions, Discoveries, and National/International Current Affairs (Sports, Summits, Awards, and political updates).

#### Basic English (Category I Only — Class 12 Level)

\* Advanced Grammar, Structural Sentence Transformations, Advanced Sentence Error Isolation.  
\* Vocabulary Usage, Complex Idioms, and Contextual Word Choice.  
\* Analytical Reading Comprehension passages and critical syntax correction.

#### Logical Reasoning (Category I Only — Class 12 Level)

Verbal Reasoning: Alphabet Series, Number Series, Analogies, Group Classification, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense tests, and Logical Deductions.  
Non-Verbal Reasoning: Pattern Completion tasks, Figure Matrix analyses, Mirror Images, Embedded Figures identification, and spatial manipulation reasoning.
